Groove Affair at Copper Face Jacks

Groove Affair at Copper Face Jacks — a night on Harcourt Street cut into vertical highlights for social. Shot down on the floor in the middle of it, edited on site, and ready to post while the queue was still going.

Nightlife content only works if it lands while the night is still the thing everyone is talking about. Filming through the peak, cutting on a mobile rig at the venue and handing over before close means the venue posts the same night — when the crowd is tagging itself, the hashtag is live, and next weekend is already being planned.

Huge thanks to Jack McGrane and the team at Copper Face Jacks for having me in and making the night an easy one to shoot.
