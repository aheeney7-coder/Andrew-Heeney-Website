Cooper International Horse Transport

A promotional film for Cooper International Horse Transport, following the fleet as it departs Tattersalls Ireland after the September sale with 40 yearlings on board, bound for Milan.

Loading day at a bloodstock sale is a narrow window — the horses move when they move, the lorries leave on schedule and nothing waits for a camera. The film was built around that: covering the loading, the yard and the convoy pulling out, then cut into a piece that carries the scale of the operation and the care that goes into moving animals of that value across Europe.

Equestrian and logistics businesses rarely have footage that shows what they actually do. A film like this works hard afterwards — on the website, in tenders, on LinkedIn and in front of the owners and trainers deciding who to trust with their horses.
